SDG 14 · Our Missions and Goals

Our Missions and Goals

Silence Is Survival

Our mission is to reduce ocean noise pollution and protect whales that rely on sound to survive. As human activity grows, the ocean is becoming louder, making it harder for marine life to communicate and hunt. This issue matters today because healthy oceans support life on Earth, including us.

  • Promote slower, quieter shipping in marine habitats.
  • Raise awareness about ocean noise pollution.
  • Support actions that protect marine ecosystems.

Lost Nets, Endless Damage

Our mission is to stop ghost nets from continuing their silent destruction in the ocean. These abandoned fishing nets trap and kill marine life long after they are lost. This issue matters today because plastic-based fishing gear is increasing, turning the ocean into a dangerous place for animals and ecosystems.

  • Support the use of biodegradable fishing gear.
  • Encourage ocean clean-up efforts.
  • Raise awareness about ghost net pollution.

Our Reefs Are Dying

Our mission is to protect coral reefs from bleaching and restore the life they support. Rising ocean temperatures are destroying these underwater cities, putting marine life and coastal communities at risk. This issue matters today because coral reefs are essential for biodiversity, protection, and future survival.

  • Reduce activities that contribute to climate change.
  • Support reef conservation and restoration projects.
  • Spread awareness about coral bleaching and ocean health.

Healing the Ocean

Our mission is to support ocean recovery by protecting marine ecosystems and encouraging global action. The ocean has the power to heal, but only if we give it the chance. This matters today because a healthy ocean supports life on Earth and offers hope for future generations.

  • Support marine protected areas (30×30 goal).
  • Reduce harmful human impact on oceans.
  • Promote awareness and action for ocean recovery.
