Whale is Warning

A massive, shadowy cargo ship looms on the surface, its hull cutting through the water. It blasts a harsh, jagged red sound wave downward. This invasive noise collides with the delicate blue echoes of a distressed mother whale and her calf, distorting their song into fractured shapes.

Ghost Nets

A massive, forgotten "ghost net" drifts like a giant, suffocating spiderweb. It is tangled over ancient, silent rock formations

Bleached Reefs

The environmental collapse hits the coral reefs. We focus on a coral garden that is rapidly dying. What was once vibrant is now a ghostly, skeletal white. The warming water has driven away all life.

A Call to Action

Action is taken. Returning underwater, we see a team of divers. Crucially, they are led by the young character from the cliffside.

Listening to the Echoes

The ocean is not silent; it is a symphony of soft, glowing sound. We observe this magical, aquatic language as it travels through the dark water.

The Community Rebuilds

True change happens through collective effort. Under a bright, sun-drenched surface, a diverse group of people including the young protagonist and the elderly lighthouse keeper work as a team.
