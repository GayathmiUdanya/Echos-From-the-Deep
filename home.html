What We're Working Toward

From bleached reefs to silent seas, our goals target the most urgent threats facing the ocean today — with clear, measurable actions that communities and governments can take right now.

  • 30% reduction in coastal carbon emissions by 2030
  • Retrieve 500 tonnes of ghost fishing gear annually
  • Establish 10 protected quiet ocean corridors
