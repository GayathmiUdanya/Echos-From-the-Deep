SDG 14 · Our Missions and Goals

Our Missions and Goals

01
Noise Pollution

Silence Is Survival

Our mission is to reduce ocean noise pollution and protect whales that rely on sound to survive. The ocean is becoming louder, disrupting ancient communication, navigation, and the hunt for food.

  • Promote slower, quieter shipping in marine habitats
  • Raise awareness about ocean noise pollution
  • Support actions that protect marine ecosystems
02
Marine Debris

Lost Nets, Endless Damage

Our mission is to stop ghost nets from their silent destruction. Abandoned fishing nets trap and kill marine life long after they are lost, drifting unseen for decades through the deep.

  • Support the use of biodegradable fishing gear
  • Encourage ocean clean-up efforts
  • Raise awareness about ghost net pollution
03
Reef Crisis

Our Reefs Are Dying

Our mission is to protect coral reefs from bleaching and restore the life they support. Rising temperatures are destroying these underwater cities, threatening biodiversity and coastal communities.

  • Reduce activities that contribute to climate change
  • Support reef conservation and restoration projects
  • Spread awareness about coral bleaching and ocean health
04
Recovery

Healing the Ocean

Our mission is to support ocean recovery by protecting marine ecosystems and encouraging global action. The ocean has the power to heal — but only if we give it the chance it deserves.

  • Support marine protected areas (30×30 goal)
  • Reduce harmful human impact on oceans
  • Promote awareness and action for ocean recovery
