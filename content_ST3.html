Introduction — Life Does Not Give Up Easily

The coral reefs are the New York city of the underwater world. They are built with millions of skyscrapers, the corals with billions of travelers, the fishes zooming past one another.

The dream city, lying below the deep blue waters, not seen by many, yet home to millions, fading by the passing second, but no one is questioning why?

Prior to turning bone white, the coral reefs illuminate a haunting blue or a purple shade of glow. To casual observer, at most it may look like a party, joyful and burst of bright colours. But scientists call this the “fluorescent scream”. It is the coral’s last scream for help to a produce a chemical sunscreen to protect themselves from the rising temperatures.

The city is not just fading

Its putting on its brightest, saddest smile before bidding the final goodbyes.

Problem — The Decline of Coral Reefs

1995 Year the Cabo Pulmo community banned all fishing
463% Increase in total fish biomass within a decade
30% Of world oceans targeted for protection by 2030

Cabo Pulmo National Park in Mexico serves as the ultimate proof of hope. After the local community recognised their reef was dying and banned fishing in 1995, the results were miraculous. Within just ten years, the reef's total fish biomass increased by a staggering 463% (Aburto-Oropeza et al., 2011). The "echo" returned, and a dead reef became one of the most robust marine reserves on Earth.

When we answer the call, the ocean answers back with life.

The 30×30 Vision

The global community is now pushing for the "30×30" goal — protecting 30% of the world's oceans by 2030. By supporting Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) and reducing industrial pressure, we give the ocean the space it needs to breathe. The warnings are clear, but so is the solution.

  • Support and fund Marine Protected Areas in your region and internationally.
  • Advocate for the 30×30 global target with your local representatives.
  • Reduce seafood consumption from unsustainable sources — choose MSC-certified products.
